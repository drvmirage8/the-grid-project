Frequently asked questions

What happens after I pay?

Your painted designs would appear on the grid for everyone to see, for as long as this site is running. If you added a link, clicking your square takes visitors straight to it.

Is my square really mine to keep?

Yes, Once you reserved and bought your square, it’s yours, with no expiry and renewal fees. It is intended by me to keep this site running indefinitely, but it isn’t a guarantee outside of my control if such events do occur that cause the site shutdown. That risk is accepted as part of buying a square here.

Can someone else buy a square I already own?

No. Once bought, No one else can claim your square or bid for it.

Can I change my design after buying?

Not currently. What is painted before the purchase would stay as it is after purchase.

How do I know my payment actually went through?

You’ll be taken through Stripe’s checkout just like any other checkout done online. Once payment is confirmed, your square appears on the grid, which is your confirmation.

Is this a real charity donation, or just a claim?

Once the 15,000 square goal is reached, I’ll personally donate half of everything raised to the World Food Programme through their official site, and post proof of the donation above.

What's the minimum I can buy?

One square is enough to be on the grid. There’s no minimum block size.

Something went wrong, who do I contact?

Reach out directly using the contact info below.

Privacy

What this site stores. When you claim a square, the site stores the position and size of your block, the colors you painted, the optional name and link you enter, and a record of the payment (the amount and a Stripe transaction reference). Everything except the payment record is displayed publicly on the grid. This site does not store your email address, billing details, or card details.

Payments. Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card and billing details are entered on Stripe's checkout page and handled by Stripe under its own privacy policy. They never pass through or are stored on this site.

Abuse prevention. When you start a checkout, your IP address is stored temporarily to limit repeated attempts. It is deleted automatically within 24 hours.

Service providers. This site runs on Supabase (database) and Vercel (hosting). As with any web host, these providers process technical data such as IP addresses and request logs in order to deliver the site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and a code library from jsDelivr, so those providers also receive your IP address when a page loads. This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or advertising tools, and it does not sell or share personal data for marketing. Stripe's checkout page may set its own cookies.

Your content and permanence. Anything you paint, name, or link is public. Once a payment is confirmed, your square is final and is not removed or edited at your request. The only exceptions are a square that breaks the content rules, a payment that is reversed through a refund or card dispute, and the case where two people pay for overlapping squares at the same moment, where the second payment is refunded in full. For questions about your data, email contact@thegridproject.net. Nothing here limits any rights you have under applicable law.

Content rules

Squares may not be used for illegal content, hate speech, harassment, adult content, malware or phishing links, or someone else's copyrighted work, trademark, name, or likeness used without their permission. I reserve the right to remove any square that breaks these rules, without a refund.

No intentional griefing

Purchases made with the goal of fragmenting the grid or blocking others from claiming a larger area, whether that's scattered single squares or any other pattern with the same intent, aren't a permitted use of this site. More broadly, anything that appears intended to disrupt the grid or other users isn't either. I reserve the right to remove squares used this way, without a refund, to keep the grid fair and usable for everyone.

Refunds

All sales are final once your square is confirmed on the grid. Because your square would appear on the grid immediately, you agree that any statutory withdrawal period for online purchases is waived at purchase. If two people pay for overlapping squares at the same moment, the second payment is refunded automatically in full. If something else went wrong with your payment, please contact me.
